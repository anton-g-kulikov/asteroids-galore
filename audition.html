Sound audition

The game's music and every sound, played by the game's own code. The numbers are in src/music/score.ts and src/audio/sounds.ts; change one and this page reloads with it.

Music

The game's own music, driven the way a run drives it. Start a run, then let the speed rise or set it by hand: the bass comes in at 1.3×, the drums at 1.7×, the arpeggio at 2.0×, at the next chord change. The lead melody comes last, from 2.2×, at the top of an eight-bar half once the arpeggio has been playing. To hear it soon, set the speed to 2.6: the lead starts within about 30 seconds, plays 32 bars (fading out over the last two), then rests 16. A one-bar rest of one instrument comes about every 30 seconds. From mission 2 (Into a wormhole, then Next mission) the bass is in at once.

Sound effects

v2 plays every event on the soundtrack's own instruments, on the chord the music is on (A minor with no music: start a run above to hear the other chords). It is what the game plays once Tone.js has loaded, a moment after the first tap. v1 is the set before it, which the game plays until then. The raw sounds at the bottom are v1's.
