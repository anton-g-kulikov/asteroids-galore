# Lightboat — story script, second draft

**Spoilers, and a draft.** First written on 2026-11-22; this draft follows the Game Producer's review of the same day, which the owner has read. Nothing here is built. The frame it follows is the "Story" section of `game-design.md`.

This is every transmission of a first playthrough, in the order a player meets them. Nothing is said while flying: each block below is one stop, shown on the story's own screen after the results.

## What changed from the first draft

- **The usual line is now the one for someone missed.** From mission 01 every third rescue is a pair built so that one crew is left, so nearly every player misses someone. The line for a clean mission is now the rare one.
- **The Foreman no longer states a rule the game does not have.** The first draft had the drill run faster for every one of the night shift taken out. In the game, speed follows the distance flown. The line now says the drill runs faster the longer the Pilot stays, which is true.
- **A destroyed boat is recovered with the Pilot,** and the other two answers the owner could give each have their line.
- **A fourth ending,** for the Pilot who stays on and still leaves many behind.
- **No stop has more than three transmissions,** and lines are cut to about 48 characters, the Producer's estimate of what fits across the field. One survivor and three of the Surveyor's lines went.
- **No line gives a count of shields.**
- **The Lantern's last message is from the field,** not from home, so the endings agree with what it says after mission 05.

## What this draft assumes

Five choices are not made yet. The script needs an answer to each, so it uses the writer's proposal and marks where it matters.

| Assumed | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| A destroyed boat means flying the mission again, in the same boat; the crew aboard are lost | [When a boat is destroyed](#when-a-boat-is-destroyed) |
| The story has its own screen, with up to three transmissions | Every stop |
| Going home after mission 03 is offered | [After mission 03](#after-mission-03--outside-m03n) |
| The choice of boat is told, as a choice about people and risk | After missions 01, 03 and 05 |
| The wormholes are the mine's haul roads, and the Lantern's list stops at `m03n` | After missions 02 and 03 |

## Who speaks

| Signs as | Who |
|---|---|
| `LANTERN` | The Watch: the tender's officer on watch. By the book, and wants the boat back |
| `SURVEYOR, m00n` | The First: the first person the Pilot delivers, who stays by the radio and keeps the names. Here that is a surveyor from `m00n`. If the first person delivered comes from a later moon, these lines are theirs and they sign by their own job and moon |
| `FOREMAN, m06n` | The night shift's foreman, heard faintly from mission 04 |
| Anyone else | A survivor who speaks once, after the mission they were saved in |

- **Until someone has been delivered there is no First.** The Lantern then says what the First would have known, read off the company's sheet.
- `[CALLSIGN]` is the player's generated callsign. `[N]` is a number the game fills in.

## Before mission 00

> **LANTERN TO [CALLSIGN]**

## Endings

Four. The way out of `m06n` leads home, so the Pilot always comes in at home. The Lantern was ordered out after mission 05: if the Pilot takes the first way out, it has left as ordered and is home to meet them; if the Pilot stays on, it disobeys and holds its place outside `m06n` until they are through.

"Few left behind" is the Producer's starting value: no more than seven missed in the whole run, one per mission. It needs checking against real or scripted runs, because the pairs force misses.

### The list

The Pilot went home after mission 03.

The Surveyor says nothing. The roll is shown with the night shift's rows blank.

### Lost at the Heart

Not in this draft. With a destroyed boat flown again there is no way to lose the story. It returns if the owner chooses either of the other two rules below.

## When a boat is destroyed

Assumed: the mission is flown again in the same boat, which the Lantern recovers with the Pilot. In any mission from 00 to 05:

> **LANTERN**
> We have you, [CALLSIGN], and the boat.
> The [N] aboard are gone.

- *If nobody was aboard:* the second line is "Nobody else was aboard."

> **SURVEYOR, m00n**
> I've marked them.
> The rest are still calling.

In mission 06 the Foreman answers first:

> **FOREMAN, m06n** *(faint)*
> We saw your light go out.
> Don't come back for us.

> **SURVEYOR, m00n**
> Go back.

What the Lantern would say under the other two rules:

| Rule | The Lantern's line | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| The same boat again (assumed) | "We have you, [CALLSIGN], and the boat. / The [N] aboard are gone." | The mission is flown again |
| One fewer on the rail | "We have you, [CALLSIGN]. The eight is gone. / Two boats left on the rail." | The mission is flown again in a boat that is left. When the last boat is lost the story ends: "That was our last boat. / [N] people got out before your light went out." |
| The story ends, as in arcade | "[N] people got out before your light went out. / They're asking for your callsign." | The ending "Lost at the Heart", in whichever mission it happens |

"One fewer on the rail" also needs a rule for which boats are on the rail to begin with, since today the eight and the twelve are only taken at a stop. That is a rules question, not the script's.

## When nobody was delivered

At any stop where the boat came through empty, the survivor's transmission is replaced by:

> **LANTERN**
> No one aboard.
> They're still calling.

## What to look at when reviewing

- **The three voices.** Whether the Watch, the Surveyor and the Foreman sound like three different people.
- **The second turn.** The Foreman now says only that the drill is winning and that staying makes it worse. That is true to the game, and quieter than the first draft. If the owner wants the stronger version, where taking the night shift out speeds the drill, it has to become a rule of story mode first.
- **The two stayed-on endings.** Whether "The long way home" reads as an ending in its own right and not as a lesser prize.
- **Line length.** About 48 characters a line is an estimate. It needs measuring on the story's screen once that is drawn.
- **What is missing on purpose:** the lines for a second playthrough, the pools of shorter lines for later runs, and anything said on the story's roll.
